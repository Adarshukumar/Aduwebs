A useful
method.

The process changes with the problem, but the principles stay consistent: understand the context, make the idea tangible, test the risky part first, and refine only what creates value.

01 / Discover

Frame the real problem

Talk to people, inspect the system, map constraints, and define what a useful outcome actually looks like.

02 / Make

Prototype the behavior

Move from abstract decisions to something testable—early enough to learn and bold enough to explore.

03 / Refine

Polish the signal

Improve hierarchy, interaction, performance, and edge cases until the experience feels clear and dependable.
