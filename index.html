04 How I work

A useful
method.

The process changes with the problem, but the principles stay consistent: understand the context, make the idea tangible, test the risky part first, and refine only what creates value.

01 / Discover

Frame the real problem

Talk to people, inspect the system, map the constraints, and define what a useful outcome actually looks like — before drawing a single screen.

Research · Mapping · Priorities
02 / Make

Prototype the behaviour

Move from abstract decisions to something testable — early enough to learn, bold enough to explore, real enough to break.

Design · Build · Test
03 / Refine

Polish the signal

Improve hierarchy, interaction, performance, and edge cases until the experience feels clear, dependable, and quietly delightful.

Detail · Performance · Ship
